Simultaneously, variable- and polarity-scrambled 3-CNF encodings of single-expander TSA and F2-homological XOR3-MAJ3 formulas defeat pure CDCL (Kissat 4.0.4, CaDiCaL 1.9.5) and CDCL+XOR (CryptoMiniSat 5), all three of which suffer exponential blowup and hit TIMEOUT_60s starting at V ≥ 48 (1,608 variables / 2,304 clauses) even with zero disjunctive selector guards (|S| = 0).

This note documents v0.3.0 of certified-arith-preproc (veripb_veronese_prg.py + proofs/veronese_prg_soundness.lean), resolving Limitation #2 for degree-2 Goldreich PRG predicates and rigorously delimiting the degree-3/4 frontier (v0.4):

  2.   3. 1. Related Work & Cook–Reckhow Positioning

  • Goldreich’s PRG, Algebraic Degree & Linearization Thresholds: Goldreich [1] (2000), Alekhnovich [2] (FOCS 2003 / TOC 2005), Bogdanov and Qiao [3] (RANDOM 2009), and Applebaum, Barak, and Wigderson [4] (STOC 2010) showed that 5-local PRGs based on degree-2 predicates (TSA = x1 ⊕ x2 ⊕ x3 ⊕ x4x5 or XOR-MAJ3) resist DPLL/Resolution (2Ω(n) lower bounds) and degree-1 F2 Gaussian elimination, while Schoenebeck [5] (FOCS 2008) proved linear-degree Ω(n) Lasserre / Sum-of-Squares lower bounds on random sparse XOR/CSP expanders.
  • Why Both CDCL and CryptoMiniSat 5 Collapse at |S| = 0:
    • In v0.2, CryptoMiniSat 5 solved standalone Tseitin140 in 1.85 s and only collapsed when disjoined with PHP across a selector cut S.
    • In single-expander Goldreich_TSA and Homological_XOR3_MAJ3 (|S| = 0), every local block has empty degree-1 vanishing ideal (I1(Bv) = {0}). Thus CryptoMiniSat 5 extracts zero linear XOR clauses, while restricting quadratic/majority variables still leaves a 3-regular Tseitin expander requiring 2Ω(V) Resolution steps (Kissat 4.0.4, CaDiCaL 1.9.5).
  • F2-Simplicial Homology Without Gate Collisions: Viewing MAJ3(xa, xb, xc) = xaxb ⊕ xaxc ⊕ xbxc (mod 2) as the 1-skeleton boundary ∂{a, b, c} ∈ Λ2([n]), any closed 2-cycle C of triangles (∂C = 0 (mod 2), e.g. disjoint K4 boundaries) satisfies ⊕T ∈ C MAJ3(T) ≡ 0 (mod 2) with zero repeated 3-tuples, defeating syntactic gate-congruence closure (Biere et al. [6], SAT 2024) while remaining polynomially refutable in VeriPB 3.0 [7, 8] via shared Veronese witnesses.

2. Blind Degree-2 Veronese VeriPB 3.0 Calculus (veripb_veronese_prg.py)

Input: Variable-permuted, polarity-scrambled, clause-shuffled 3-CNF (n_vars, m clauses)
1. Logged (1,1)-chain unrolling (`_unroll_11_chains_logged`):
     Eliminates Tseitin 3-CNF splitting chains via `pol id_p id_n + [s] ;`.
2. Optional primal cut discovery (`discover_primal_vertex_cut`, |S| <= 6):
     - Single-expander PRGs yield S = None (|S| = 0, single fiber ()).
     - Hybrid formulas (e.g. PHP OR_S Homological_XOR3_MAJ3) split into 2^|S| fibers.
3. Per-fiber degree-<=2 Veronese extraction (`_extract_veronese_rm2_records`):
     For each unrolled block supp = (v_1, ..., v_k) (1 <= k <= 7) with >= 2^{k-1} clauses:
       - Compute exact O(k * 2^k) F_2 Mobius / ANF transform (or RM(2, k) vanishing ideal).
       - Reject blocks of algebraic degree >= 3 with empty RM(2, k) ideal (e.g. XOR-MAJ_5).
       - Emit record: L_i(x) ^ Q_i(x) == c_0^(i) (mod 2) in Veronese coordinates RM(2, n).
4. Sparse GF(2) RREF in RM(2, n) with minimal core tracking:
     Eliminate over coordinates {(u,)} U {(u, w) : u < w}; extract minimal inconsistent E*.
5. Lazy VeriPB 3.0 (`pol` + `red`, ZERO `rup`) realization on E*:
     a) Shared Veronese product witnesses p_{u,w} <-> (x_u & x_w) ONLY for (u,w) in U_{i in E*} Q_i:
          and_11: red 1 ~x_u 1 ~x_w 1 x_p >= 1 : x_p -> 1 ;
          and_0x: red 1 x_u 1 ~x_p >= 1 : x_p -> 0 ;
          and_x0: red 1 x_w 1 ~x_p >= 1 : x_p -> 0 ;
     b) Fold linearized block parity z_1 ^ ... ^ z_r == c_0^(i) via (r - 2) unconditional 3-XOR
        `red` steps (`_realize_parity3_bounds`), reducing the block residual to two B = 1 clauses
        on (s_{r-2}, z_r), derived over the 2^k-cube on `supp` via exact clause halving (`2 d`).
     c) Even-multiplicity Gocht-Nordstrom CG halving (`sum_and_halve`):
        Cancels all x_u, all shared p_{u,w}, all folding s_{i,t}, and all MAJ_3 witnesses w_e
        for arbitrary even multiplicities 2*M_z in Z, deriving G >= 1 (or 0 >= 1 at |S| = 0).
Output: Complete `.pbp` certificate verified by `veripb 3.0.2 --cnf`.

5. Honest Limitations & Delimited Frontier for v0.4

  1. Higher-Degree Algebraic Barriers (deg ≥ 3: Cubic XOR3-AND3 & Goldreich XOR3-MAJ5): As proved mechanically in xor_maj5_no_deg2_annihilator and measured in Table 2, predicates whose local vanishing ideal has trivial intersection with RM(2, k) (nullity == 0) yield zero equations in degree-2 Veronese space. Extending certification to XOR3-AND3 and XOR3-MAJ5 requires higher-order Veronese lifts RM(d, n) (d ∈ {3, 4}) or sparse Macaulay syzygy cascades, paying O(nd) coordinates unless bounded by hypergraph locality.
  2. Sub-Quadratic Random PRGs Without Homological 2-Cycles (m ≪ C(n, 2), ∂C ≠ 0): When m < n + n(n − 1)/2 random 5-tuples lack a closed F2-simplicial 2-cycle in Λ2([n]), the linearized RM(2, n) system has fewer rows than columns and is rank-deficient, matching Schoenebeck’s Ω(n)-degree SoS lower bound [5].
  3. External SAT Competition Benchmarks & Rust Port (v0.4): All v0.1–v0.3 families remain synthetic scrambled 3-CNFs; porting the proof logger to Rust and evaluating coverage on industrial / SAT Competition UNSAT benchmarks is the next engineering milestone.
