Abstract

Wire-eliminated disjunctive formulas of the form PHPph ∨S TseitinG—where a small selector cut S switches between a Pigeonhole Principle core and a Tseitin parity core over 3-regular graphs, and long guarded clauses are flattened into anonymous, variable- and polarity-scrambled 3-CNF—defeat both state-of-the-art Conflict-Driven Clause Learning (CDCL) solvers and CDCL+XOR solvers. Pure CDCL (Kissat 4.0.4, CaDiCaL 1.9.5) faces exponential resolution lower bounds on both PHP and Tseitin, while CDCL+XOR (CryptoMiniSat 5) is blinded by (1, 1) wire-elimination chains and collapses on the PHP branch.

This technical note documents v0.2.0 of certified-arith-preproc, introducing a blind disjunctive VeriPB 3.0 proof-emitting calculus (veripb_interpolant.py) paired with a universal ∀n formalization in Lean 4.36.0 (proofs/disjunctive_interpolant_soundness.lean):

  1. End-to-End VeriPB 3.0.2 Certification: Achieves 13/13 s VERIFIED UNSATISFIABLE proofs across |S| ∈ {0, 2, 3} (11 scaling/multi-selector instances and 2 standalone |S| = 0 ablations), scaling up to 16,656 variables and 29,664 clauses (PHP24_23_OR2_Tseitin52) in 3,089.92 ms total (2,972.84 ms Python emit + 117.08 ms veripb 3.0.2 check) where Kissat 4.0.4, CaDiCaL 1.9.5, and CryptoMiniSat 5 all time out at 60 s.
  2. Zero-False-Positive SAT Rejection: Cleanly rejects 4/4 satisfiable control instances (verified SAT by CaDiCaL 1.9.5) with zero false UNSAT certificates.
  3. Universal ∀n Lean 4.36.0 Soundness: Mechanically verifies 17 declarations across 7 theorem groups with 0 sorryAx and standard Lean kernel axioms only, closing Limitation #4 of Technical Note v0.1 for the disjunctive calculus.

1. Related Work & Proof-Complexity Positioning

  • Feasible Interpolation & Cutting-Planes Separations: Pudlák [1] (JSL 1997) and Krajíček [2] (JSL 1997) established feasible interpolation for Resolution and Cutting Planes, showing that split formulas A(x, z) ∧ B(y, z) admit monotone interpolating circuits when shared variables carry opposite polarities. Hrubeš and Pudlák [3] (FOCS 2017) and Garg, Göös, Kamath, and Sokolov [4] (STOC 2018) proved sharp monotone lower bounds for Cutting Planes and communication protocols when non-monotone parity guards link disjoint hard components.
  • Why Both CDCL and CDCL+XOR Collapse on Wire-Eliminated PHPph ∨S TseitinG:
    • Clausal Congruence & Resolution (Kissat 4.0.4, CaDiCaL 1.9.5): Biere et al. [5] (SAT 2024) recover AND/XOR/ITE gates via clausal congruence closure, but PHP and Tseitin share no congruent gates and both require exponential-size resolution refutations.
    • Gauss-Jordan XOR (CryptoMiniSat 5): While CryptoMiniSat 5 solves standalone Tseitin140 on 3-regular expanders in 1,854.25 ms via Gaussian elimination over F2, it collapses once disjoined with PHP: (1, 1) Tseitin splitting hides parity supports behind auxiliary chains, and the PHP fibers contain no XOR structure.
  • Pseudo-Boolean & Parity Proof Logging: Gocht and Nordström [6] (AAAI 2021) introduced compact parity certification in VeriPB via witness-bearing extension variables, generalized in VeriPB 3.0 by Bogaerts et al. [7] (JAIR 2023), while Szeider [8] (PBLean / pb-catcher, 2026) formalized PB certificate checking in Lean 4. Our pipeline complements these works by recovering the hidden separator, clique structure, and parity system blindly from scrambled 3-CNF and emitting a unified polynomial-size VeriPB 3.0 proof.

2. Blind Disjunctive VeriPB 3.0 Calculus (veripb_interpolant.py)

Input: Anonymous Scrambled DIMACS 3-CNF (n_vars, m clauses)
1. Global Logged (1,1)-Chain Unrolling (`_unroll_11_chains_logged`):
     Iteratively cancel occurrence-(1,1) Tseitin splitting variables via
     `pol id_p id_n + ;` (disjoint) or `pol id_p id_n + s ;` (overlapping).
2. Pigeonhole-Anchored Primal Vertex-Cut Discovery (`discover_primal_vertex_cut`):
     Among the first (max_cut + 1) minimum-degree vertices in G_primal, at least
     one anchor u lies outside S (|S| <= max_cut). Probe S_cand = N(u) ∩ N(v)
     for v not in N(u) via fast BFS in O(|V| * deg) time.
3. Per-Fiber Certification for each b in {0, 1}^|S| (guard G = sum_{t in S} l_t(b)):
     a) Project active clauses onto fiber b; run fiber-local (1,1) unrolling
        keeping cut variables S protected.
     b) Guarded Chvatal-Gomory Clique AMO + PHP (`_try_certify_guarded_php`):
        For each hole clique Q_j (|Q_j| = m_q), derive inductively for m = 2..m_q-1:
          I_{m+1} := ceil( ((m - 1) * I_m + sum_{i=0}^{m-1} M_{i,m}) / m )
                  -> m*G + sum_{i=0}^{m} ~q_i >= m   (`pol ... m d ;`)
        Sum p guarded ALO clauses + h clique AMO inequalities and divide by c_guard
        to derive G >= 1 (0 extension variables).
     c) Guarded MAJ_3 `red`-Witness Parity (`_try_certify_guarded_parity`):
        Run sparse GF(2) RREF tracking row-combination bitmasks; emit witnesses ONLY
        for equations in the minimal inconsistent subset E*:
          R_1: y1 + y2 + y3 + 2*~w >= 2 : x_w -> 0 ;
          R_2: ~y1 + ~y2 + ~y3 + 2*w >= 2 : x_w -> 1 ;
          L_i := ceil((R_2 + K_i) / 2),  U^- := ceil((2*(L_1+L_2+L_3) + K_4) / 3)
        Halve sum(U^+) and sum(U^-) via `2 d`, add to cancel all x_v and w_e, and
        divide by tot_m to derive G >= 1.
4. Boolean Cube Halving Reassembly (`collapse_cube`):
     Pair opposing fibers via 2^|S| - 1 steps `pol id0 id1 + 2 d ;` -> `0 >= 1`.
Output: Complete `.pbp` proof verified by `veripb 3.0.2 --cnf`.

2.1 Exact Algebraic Identities Behind the Proof Logger

  • Guarded Chvátal–Gomory Clique Step: Given (m − 1)G + Σi=1m x̄i ≥ m − 1 and m binary guarded clauses G + x̄i + x̄m+1 ≥ 1, the linear combination (m − 1)Im + Σi=1m Mi,m+1 produces uniform literal weight m on every x̄1, …, x̄m+1 and guard/RHS weight m2 − m + 1. Dividing by m yields ⌈(m2 − m + 1)/m⌉ = m, preserving exact 0–1 literal coefficients without slack growth.
  • Exact Integer-Parity Identity via MAJ3: On all 8 Boolean assignments of (y1, y2, y3) ∈ {0, 1}3, setting w = MAJ3(y1, y2, y3) satisfies y1 + y2 + y3 − 2w = y1 ⊕ y2 ⊕ y3 identically over Z. In normalized positive-literal coordinates, shifting negative even weights −2|mv|xv = 2|mv|x̄v − 2|mv| adds an even constant to the RHS, commuting with Chvátal–Gomory division by 2 (⌈(B + 2K)/2⌉ = ⌈B/2⌉ + K).

5. Limitations & Transparency

  1. Bounded Separator Width (|S| ≤ 6): Full Boolean cube enumeration incurs a 2|S| multiplier in proof length and emit time. Wide separators (|S| ≫ 6) require tree-decomposition or monotone LP interpolation rather than explicit fiber enumeration.
  2. Non-Separable Expander Topologies: Cryptographic PRGs such as Goldreich XOR-MAJ3 form a single high-expansion primal component without small vertex cuts and remain hard outside bounded-width regimes.
  3. Synthetic Benchmark Scope: All instances in Tables 1–3 are generated synthetically and scrambled; evaluation on industrial SAT Competition benchmarks is left to future work.
  4. AI-Assisted Research Note: Developed with multi-agent AI-assisted theorem proving and coding workflows, with every claim independently validated by local execution of lean 4.36.0, veripb 3.0.2, kissat 4.0.4, CaDiCaL 1.9.5, and CryptoMiniSat 5.
