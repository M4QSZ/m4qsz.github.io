Abstract

Conflict-Driven Clause Learning (CDCL) solvers and syntactic gate-equivalence preprocessors struggle on heterogeneous arithmetic equivalence miters presented as flat, variable- and polarity-scrambled Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF), as well as on disjunctive combinations of cardinality and parity constraints whose selector wires have been eliminated. Conversely, computer-algebraic verifiers for integer multipliers typically require gate-level And-Inverter Graphs (AIGs) and emit proofs in specialized algebraic calculi (PAC/LPAC) separate from pseudo-Boolean cutting-planes checkers.

Technical Note v0.4 left open two concrete preprocessing barriers—lifted gadget compositions F ∘ g (Lifted_PHP12_11_OverlappingXOR3 at 52,587 variables, Lifted_PHP11_10_Deg2Sponge_MAJ3 at 356,789 variables, and Lifted_PHP65_o_Keccak4L_Scrambled) and linear-stretch Goldreich PRGs / integer carry miters in native Rust—alongside open theoretical questions at the frontier of proof complexity. Version v0.5.0 resolves these items across two integrated tiers: (1) Unified Native Rust VeriPB 3.0 Engine (cap-veronese-rs v0.5.0, ATAQUE-01, 02, 04): Local-Intersection Zassenhaus & Directional Derivative Isolation (lifted_zassenhaus.rs), Binomial Ideal Lift (binom_macaulay.rs), and Native Rust Z-Adder Telescoping & Truncated Mod-2k Hensel Peeling (z_adder.rs); and (2) Empirical & Formal Frontier Laboratories (ATAQUE-03, 05, 06, 07, 08): Schoenebeck Random 3-SAT Phase Transition, Krajíček’s circuit-rewriting relation ≈, Non-Commutative IPS over Witt rings Wk(F2) ≅ Z/2kZ, VeriPB 3.0 Dominance G1, and Constructive Bug Extraction.

Finite rule-level soundness of all underlying boolean and integer rewrite identities is mechanically checked in Lean 4.36.0 (0 sorryAx), while end-to-end evaluations certify scrambled multiplier miters (6x6 to 16x16 bits, v0.1), unified 11-benchmark corpus (v0.5), G1 dominance benchmarks, and clean rejection of 4/4 SAT controls.

1. Introduction & Related Work

Modern SAT solving relies heavily on preprocessing and inprocessing to recover high-level structure lost during Tseitin encoding:

  • Clausal Congruence Closure: Biere, Fazekas, Fleury, and Froleyks (SAT 2024, integrated in Kissat 4.0.4) extract AND, XOR, and ITE gates directly from CNF and merge isomorphic gate outputs via congruence closure with clausal proof logging. While highly effective on combinational miters sharing internal subcircuits, congruence closure alone cannot equate structurally heterogeneous architectures such as a ripple/schoolbook multiplier against a scrambled Wallace-tree multiplier, where internal partial-product sums share no equivalent intermediate gates.
  • Algebraic Multiplier Verification: AMulet 2.2, TeluMA, and RevSCA-2.0 (Kaufmann, Biere, Beame, Nordström, TACAS 2021, DATE 2022) verify large multipliers via Gröbner-basis reduction combined with adder rewriting, certifying algebraic steps in PAC/LPAC (Pacheck 2.0; Hofstadler & Kaufmann; Saccomani et al., FMCAD 2026). However, these tools assume AIG/circuit netlists rather than anonymous scrambled 3-CNF, and live in an algebraic proof ecosystem separate from pseudo-Boolean cutting planes.
  • Pseudo-Boolean & Parity Certification (VeriPB): Gocht and Nordström (AAAI 2021) showed how to certify F2 Gaussian elimination efficiently inside VeriPB using extension variables, and Koops et al. (CP 2025) established practical LP/Farkas and cutting-planes proof logging for RoundingSat and Sat4j backed by VeriPB 3.0 and CakePB.
  • Auxiliary Variable Synthesis & Relaxations: Structured Bounded Variable Addition (SBVA; Haberlandt, Green, Heule, SAT 2023) introduces extension variables syntactically via clause-biclique heuristics, whereas combined linear/affine relaxations (BLP + AIP; Brakensiek et al. 2019; Ciardo & Živný 2023) solve promise CSPs without synthesizing nonlinear extension definitions or coupling cutting planes with degree-absorbing Macaulay reductions.

Contributions. Bridging the gap between blind CNF preprocessing, algebraic reasoning, and VeriPB 3.0 cutting-planes certification, we contribute:

  1. Direct VeriPB 3.0 Certification of Scrambled CNF Multiplier Miters (veripb_emitter.py / z_adder.rs): Without AIGs, variable ordering, or PAC checkers, our engine extracts integer Witt adder equations directly from scrambled 3-CNF and logs complete VeriPB 3.0 cutting-planes refutations (rup, pol, and integer division d) checked by veripb 3.0.2.
  2. Nonlinear Veronese Annihilator Synthesis (veronese_annihilator.py / lifted_zassenhaus.rs): A semantic generalization of auxiliary variable introduction that lifts local falsification sets to νd : F2n → F2C(n, ≤d) and collapses exponential syzygy subspaces in O(|V| · dim2) time via directional Boolean derivatives.
  3. Monotone Coupled Sparse Macaulay & Guarded Cutting Planes (macaulay_cp.py / binom_macaulay.rs): Exploits universal RM(2, 3) cubic cancellation on colliding 3-variable supports alongside monotone guarded clique, pair-carry, and primal vertex-cut deductions.

2. Unified Architecture (v0.1–v0.5)

2.1 Blind Witt–Hensel Extraction and VeriPB 3.0 Logging (z_adder.rs)

Given an anonymous 3-CNF formula over x1, …, xn:

  1. Local Gate & Congruence Scan: Grouping clauses of width ≤ 3 over 3-variable triples (v0, v1, v2) identifies all deterministic 2-input boolean functions o = g(u, w) invariant under arbitrary input/output negations. Congruent outputs o1 ≡ o2± on shared inputs (u, w) are merged and certified in VeriPB 3.0 by deriving the two binary clauses o1 + o2 ≥ 1 and o1 + o2 ≥ 1 via 2-variable cube RUP elimination (derive_clause_over_cube, 7 lines of rup/pol ... 2 d per clause). Shortened 2-clauses induced by substitution are synchronized into the VeriPB core/derived database via 1-step rup so unit propagation trails remain aligned.
  2. Witt Half-Adder & Disjoint-Carry Full-Adder Synthesis:
    • When a pair (u, w) drives both an XOR/XNOR wire s and a quadratic AND/OR/NAND/NOR carry wire c, polarity-normalized literals (ũ, w̃, s̃, c̃) satisfy the exact Z-linear identity ũ + w̃ − s̃ − 2c̃ = 0. We certify both inequalities in VeriPB 3.0 purely from width-≤3 RUP clauses (A1..A4 and D1, D2, B1):

      2((c̃ + ũ) + (c̃ + w̃) + (c̃ + s̃)) + (ũ + w̃ + s̃) ≥ 7  →3 d  ũ + w̃ + s̃ + 2c̃ ≥ 3,

      (ũ + s̃ + c̃) + (w̃ + s̃ + c̃) + (ũ + w̃ + c̃) + (c̃ ≥ 0) ≥ 3  →2 d  ũ + w̃ + s̃ + 2c̃ ≥ 2.

    • When two partial carries (c1, c2) of a full-adder feed an output gate cout, mutual exclusion ¬(c̃1 ∧ c̃2) converts the nonlinear OR into the exact integer equality c̃1 + c̃2 − c̃out = 0, certified in VeriPB via (c̃1 + c̃out) + (c̃2 + c̃out) + (c̃1 + c̃2) ≥ 3 →2 d c̃1 + c̃2 + c̃out ≥ 2.
  3. Native Rust Z-Adder Telescoping & Truncated Mod-2k Hensel Peeling (z_adder.rs): Accelerates Miter_Mult_12x12..20x20_Scrambled proof emission by 11.0×–18.7× over Python (20x20 emit drops from 27,786.8 ms to 2,527.17 ms) and certifies truncated MSB-free mod-212 miters (Trunc_Miter_12x12_low12, 287.42 ms total).

2.2 Local-Intersection Zassenhaus & Directional Derivative Isolation (lifted_zassenhaus.rs)

Replaces Python’s 100-variable global monomial cap with inverted-index local subspace intersections RM+(d, Si ∩ Sj) (|Si ∩ Sj| ≤ 11, u128 bitwise elimination) + directional Boolean derivative ΔuP ≡ 1 isolation + multi-clause prime ray rewriting (pol + red, 0 rup). Blindly certifies Lifted_PHP12_11_OverlappingXOR3 (52,587 vars, 260,594 .pbp lines) in 1.80 s total (857.35 ms Rust + 947.17 ms veripb), Lifted_PHP11_10_Deg2Sponge_MAJ3 (356,789 vars / 413,952 clauses, 2,690,342 .pbp lines) in 13.02 s total (5.20 s Rust + 7.81 s veripb, where Kissat 4.0.4 and CryptoMiniSat 5 time out), and Lifted_PHP65_o_Keccak4L_Scrambled (17,373 vars) in 681.38 ms.

2.3 Binomial Ideal Lift & Logged Case-Split Cascade (binom_macaulay.rs)

Certifies binomial ideal lifts such as (z3 ⊕ z4) · EMAJ3, interpolants like Interpolant_OR_s1s2_PHP_Goldreich (|S| = 2, 552.48 ms), and linear-stretch Goldreich_XORMAJ3_n28_m36_Scrambled (1.48 s Rust emit, 0 rup), while rejecting 4/4 SAT controls (exit = 10, 0 false positives).

2.4 VeriPB 3.0 Dominance G1 (ATAQUE-07)

Proves well-founded preorder dominance soundness in Lean 4.36.0 and verifies 16/16 G1 .pbp proofs in veripb 3.0.2, certifying Keccak_1Round_Bijection_PHP_11_10 in 139.19 ms vs Kissat 4.0.4 16,062.49 ms (115× speedup).

Limitations & Discussion

  • Where Algebraic–Polyhedral Extraction Dominates: On heterogeneous scrambled multiplier miters (12x12–20x20) and scaled wire-eliminated cardinality–parity interpolants (PHP1312 ∨ Tseitin28), Kissat 4.0.4’s Clausal Congruence Closure finds no internal equivalent gates between the schoolbook and Wallace adder trees, and both Kissat 4.0.4 and CryptoMiniSat 5 time out (>20 s), whereas our engine refutes them deterministically with 0 branches.
  • Where Native C CDCL Dominates: When the underlying pigeonhole core or seed dimension is small (PHP65, n = 28 local PRG), native C CDCL search in Kissat 4.0.4 resolves the search tree in 36–128 ms, outperforming preprocessing overhead. Likewise, on random 3-SAT UNSAT (α = 6.2), while our coupled monotone engine demonstrates an interesting theoretical 0% + 0% → 100% synergy between cutting planes and sparse degree-3 Macaulay at n = 64, standard CDCL solves n = 64 in milliseconds and asymptotic boundary-expansion lower bounds remain in force as n grows.

Consequently, the practical deployment target of our method is certified fast-path preprocessing / portfolio solving ahead of CDCL (Kissat / CaDiCaL), emitting unified VeriPB 3.0 certificates whenever arithmetic adder networks, Veronese algebraic definitions, or separated cardinality–parity components are detected.
