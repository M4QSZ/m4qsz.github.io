Abstract

In Extended Frege proofs, circuits and rewriting (arXiv:2606.13367v2, p. 11), Jan Krajíček asks whether every Circuit Frege (CF) proof of total DAG size S and k proof lines admits a polynomial-size conversion into an equational rewriting chain E0 ≈ E1 ≈ … ≈ Et = 1, where A ≈ B denotes applying a single Boolean algebra (BA) axiom at a single occurrence in the tree unwinding Fla[A]. We resolve the structural sharing, compressed-proof, and syntactic-alignment frontiers of this problem, and establish an exact boundary separating syntactic circuit sharing from pure semantic equivalence:

  1. Fan-Out ≥ 2 Unsharing & poly(S) Compilation (ATAQUE-10): Incorporating the October 2026 Datta–Dutta correction—where simultaneous rewriting across a shared gate of fan-out ≥ 2 violates A ≈ B (|C|_DAG = 4 -> 5 on (x1 &lor; &neg;x1) &land; (x1 &lor; &neg;x1))—we formalize persistent path-copying DAG unsharing (|C′|DAG ≤ |C|DAG + |p| + cba), a zero-unwinding 2-step Fla-bridge C ≈ (0 &lor; C) ≈ D, and tree-balancing yielding O(k log k) chain length.
  2. Aligned SLP kCF-Pure Compression (ATAQUE-13): Using parameterized template chains and symbolic Modus Ponens cancellation, any k-line aligned SLPCFProof compiles to a concrete ≈-chain of length t ≤ (cax + 2)k, strictly independent of intermediate DAG size S (verified up to S = 36,004 and unwound formula size 4.10 × 1018).
  3. Non-Aligned Context-SLPs via Hash-Consed Jež Recompression (ATAQUE-15): For completely misaligned linear ContextSLP hierarchies (dyadic doubling vs. period-3 or Thue–Morse grammars), canonical spine flattening toSpine and hash-consed Jež RLE + locally-consistent pair recompression decide Fla[G1] = Fla[G2] in deterministic O(poly(|G1| + |G2|)) time without tree unwinding, verified up to spine length 3 ⋅ 260 ≈ 3.46 × 1018 in 10.057 ms, yielding t ≤ (cax + 2)k non-aligned compilation.
  4. Exact Syntactic-vs-Semantic Dichotomy & ⌈log2 t⌉ Bug Localization (ATAQUE-08, ATAQUE-15): Whenever Fla[A] = Fla[B], equivalence is decidable in P and bridged in 2 universal ≈-steps; when Fla[A] ≠ Fla[B] yet A ≡Bool B, bridging requires genuine coNP tautology derivations (2 disjCompl steps for (x0 &lor; &neg;x0) vs. (x1 &lor; &neg;x1), 807 steps for XOR3 associativity). Conversely, any falsifying assignment x* for E0 locates a broken transition Ei(x*) ≠ Ei+1(x*) in ≤ ⌈log2 t⌉ queries (arXiv:2611.08305).

1. Introduction & Krajíček’s Open Problem (arXiv:2606.13367v2, p. 11)

Let LDM = {0, 1, xi, &neg;, &lor;, &land;} be the DeMorgan language and let BA be the 12 standard Boolean algebra equational axiom schemes (negZero: &neg;0 = 1; negOne: &neg;1 = 0; disjZeroLeft: 0 &lor; a = a; conjOneLeft: 1 &land; a = a; disjCompl: a &lor; &neg;a = 1; conjCompl: a &land; &neg;a = 0; disjComm, conjComm; disjAssoc, conjAssoc; disjDistrib, conjDistrib). A DeMorgan circuit C is a straight-line DAG of gates over LDM with designated output gate C.out and DAG size |C|DAG. Its tree unwinding Fla[C] (unfold C C.out) expands every gate reference into a DeMorganTerm.

For formulas s, t, single-occurrence rewriting s ≈BA t (SimBA s t) holds iff there exists a unique tree path p ∈ {L, R}* (SimBAPath p s t) where t replaces the single subformula at p by one bidirectional instance of BA (BAStep). An n-step equational chain SimBAN n s t inductively chains n single-occurrence rewrites (s = E0 ≈ E1 ≈ … ≈ En = t). Lifting to circuits, A ≈ B (ApproxDAG A B) holds iff Fla[A] ≈BA Fla[B].

The Datta–Dutta Fan-Out ≥ 2 Correction (v2, October 2026)

In arXiv:2606.13367v1, rewriting a shared DAG gate g of fan-out f ≥ 2 in-place appeared to simulate one ≈-step. As Datta and Dutta observed (acknowledged in arXiv:2606.13367v2), mutating a gate of fan-out f ≥ 2 alters f distinct occurrences in Fla[C] simultaneously and therefore violates SimBA. On C = (x1 &lor; &neg;x1) &land; (x1 &lor; &neg;x1) (|C|_DAG = 4, where g = x1 &lor; &neg;x1 has fan-out 2), simultaneous replacement yields 1 &land; 1 with fanout2_simultaneous_both_approx = false. Unsharing along path p = [0] produces C′ = (1 &land; (x1 &lor; &neg;x1)) (|C'|_DAG = 5, 4 -> 5), giving the 3-step verified chain (krajicek_ew_graph_results.json):

((x1 &lor; &neg;x1) &land; (x1 &lor; &neg;x1)) →disjCompl:fwd @ [0] (1 &land; (x1 &lor; &neg;x1)) →conjOneLeft:fwd @ [] (x1 &lor; &neg;x1) →disjCompl:fwd @ [] 1.

Three Regimes of CF -> ≈-Chain Conversion

Circuit Frege (CF) represents proof lines as circuits Ci (total size S = ∑i |Ci|DAG, line count k) and includes the structural Fla-rule C / D whenever Fla[C] = Fla[D]. We resolve three regimes of CF -> ≈-Chain:

  • (a) poly(S) DAG Proofs (ATAQUE-10): Arbitrary intra-circuit sharing and inter-line DAG proof reuse compiled via path-copying unsharing and midpoint proof-tree balancing into O(k log k) ≈-steps and poly(S) circuit size.
  • (b) Aligned SLP-Compressed kCF Proofs (ATAQUE-13): The kCF-pure regime S ≫ poly(k + |C| + |D|) where giant intermediate formulas Bi share a parameterized SLP template grammar, compiled in t ≤ (cax + 2)k steps without materializing S.
  • (c) Non-Aligned Context-SLPs vs. Pure Semantic Equivalence (ATAQUE-15): Misaligned linear ContextSLP decompositions satisfying Fla[G1] = Fla[G2] (decided in P via Jež recompression and bridged in 2 steps) versus semantically equivalent A ≡Bool B with Fla[A] ≠ Fla[B].

3. Polynomial-Time Compilers & Hash-Consed Jež Recompression

3.1 Zero-Unwinding 0 &lor; C Bridge, Path-Copying Unsharing, and O(k log k) Balancing

When a CF derivation invokes Fla[C] = Fla[D] between circuits C, D with incompatible DAG sharing (|C|_DAG ≠ |D|_DAG), unwinding Fla[C] would incur exponential blowup (8,388,601 formula nodes at m = 20 in Suite 1 of ATAQUE-10). Instead, krajicek_cf_compiler.py allocates the 2-gate circuit M := (0 &lor; C) (zeroOrCircuit C) and applies BAAxiom.disjZeroLeft backward and forward at root path []:

C →disjZeroLeft:bwd @ [] (0 &lor; C) →disjZeroLeft:fwd @ [] D.

Because Fla[0 &lor; C] = (0 &lor; Fla[C]) = (0 &lor; Fla[D]), both transitions are exact single-occurrence SimBA steps with zero unwinding. For internal BA rewrites at tree path p, persistent pathCopy clones only the |p| ancestors on p (|D'| ≤ |D| + |p| when substituting an existing gate, or ≤ |D| + |p| + c_ba before hash-consing). For CF proof DAGs with line fan-out ≥ 2, recursive midpoint balancing eliminates exponential proof-tree unwinding, yielding O(k log k) ≈-steps and poly(S) total DAG size.

3.2 SLP Template Modus Ponens Cancellation (S ≫ poly(k))

In the kCF-pure regime where S ≫ poly(k + |C| + |D|), krajicek_slp_unifier.py operates over symbolic SLP grammars with metavariable holes hole i representing unmaterialized giant subcircuits ρ(i). By tsimBAN_inst and template_modus_ponens, Modus Ponens over Bi cancels the shared template in literally +2 concrete steps (negOne + disjZeroLeft), yielding total chain length t ≤ (cax + 2)k completely independent of S.

3.3 Hash-Consed Jež Recompression on Non-Aligned ContextSLPs

When G1 and G2 are non-syntactically-aligned ContextSLPs—unary DeMorgan tree grammars built with id, atom (&neg;□, □ &lor; R, L &lor; □, □ &land; R, L &land; □), and associative composition comp—different parse hierarchies (e.g., dyadic doubling Dm+1 = Dm ∘ Dm vs. period-3 or Thue–Morse splits) share zero intermediate nonterminals. krajicek_nonaligned_slp.py decides Fla[G1] = Fla[G2] in O(poly(|G1| + |G2|)) time via recompress_unify_context_slp (Jež 2016; Lohrey 2012):

  1. Normalized CFG Extraction (_slp_to_cfg_rhs): Eliminate id rules and alias unit nonterminals in O(|G1| + |G2|), representing atomic context frames as negative terminal IDs.
  2. Exact RLE Block Compression (run_exact_rle_phase): A single bottom-up pass classifies each nonterminal as pure (a single maximal run ac) or impure (l_tok, l_cnt, mid_core, r_tok, r_cnt) and compresses maximal blocks ac to canonical tokens via a hash-consed block_dict.
  3. Locally-Consistent Pair Compression & Hash-Consed Pruning (prune_to_roots): Compute first_tok/last_tok and weighted adjacent pairs (a, b) across rule boundaries; greedily partition terminals into (L, R) maximizing cut weight; pop R-prefixes and L-suffixes out of nonterminals; merge (L × R) pairs via pair_dict; and compact reachable rules with rhs_intern.

Each round compresses the expanded spine length by a constant factor without unfolding the 2m tree, deciding equality up to spine length 3 ⋅ 260 = 3,458,764,513,820,540,928 ≈ 3.46 × 1018 in 10.057 ms.

Correctness of Non-Aligned Compilation (evalSpine_toSpine, nonaligned_ctx_cf_compression): By structural induction on K : CtxExpr, evalSpine_toSpine establishes evalSpine (toSpine K) t = evalCtx K t using evalSpine_append (toSpine (.compC k₁ k₂) = toSpine k₁ ++ toSpine k₂). Consequently, whenever recompress_unify_context_slp certifies toSpine K₁ = toSpine K₂ and u₁ = u₂, nonaligned_ctx_two_step_approx constructs the 2-step bridge SimBAN 2 (evalCtx K₁ u₁) (evalCtx K₂ u₂) via 0 &lor; C. In particular, doubling_tower_reassoc_two_step bridges arbitrary reassociations (D_m ∘ D_m) ∘ K vs. D_m ∘ (D_m ∘ K) of exponential depth ≥ 2m+1 in exactly 2 ≈-steps for every m ∈ N. Lifting inductively across CtxCFProof in krajicek_nonaligned_context_slp.lean, nonaligned_ctx_cf_compression compiles any k-line proof combining bounded-cost axiom templates (m ≤ cax), Modus Ponens cuts over giant compressed contexts (evalSpine (toSpine K_Imp) u_Imp), and non-aligned toSpine unification steps to a valid concrete chain SimBAN t .one (evalCtx K u) with:

t ≤ (cax + 2) k,

strictly independent of intermediate DAG size S and exponential tree depth 2m.

4. Syntactic-vs-Semantic Dichotomy & Constructive Bug Localization (arXiv:2611.08305)

Theorem 4 (syntactic_vs_semantic_fla_dichotomy) delineates the exact computational boundary of CF -> ≈-Chain:

  • Syntactic Fla-Equality (∈ P, 2 ≈-steps): Any non-aligned (K₁, u₁), (K₂, u₂) with toSpine K₁ = toSpine K₂ and u₁ = u₂ satisfies Fla[K₁(u₁)] = Fla[K₂(u₂)], is certified in deterministic polynomial time by Jež recompression, and bridges in 2 universal 0 &lor; C steps.
  • Pure Semantic Equivalence (coNP-hard, Non-Universal BA Steps): When Fla[A] ≠ Fla[B] despite A ≡Bool B, structural Fla sharing is powerless. For tautVar0 = (x0 &lor; &neg;x0) and tautVar1 = (x1 &lor; &neg;x1), &neg; SimBA tautVar0 tautVar1 holds (1 step is impossible); bridging requires 2 variable-eliminating disjCompl steps (x0 &lor; &neg;x0 ≈ 1 ≈ x1 &lor; &neg;x1). When variables coincide but Boolean structure differs, e.g., XOR3 associativity ((x1 ⊕ x2) ⊕ x3) vs. (x1 ⊕ (x2 ⊕ x3)), bidirectional graph search requires 807 verified BA steps.

Syntactic-vs-Semantic Dichotomy in Lean (syntactic_vs_semantic_fla_dichotomy): Explicitly, krajicek_nonaligned_context_slp.lean proves the conjunction:

  1. (Syntactic Fla-equality regime) ∀ K₁ K₂ u, toSpine K₁ = toSpine K₂ → SimBAN 2 (evalCtx K₁ u) (evalCtx K₂ u);
  2. (Semantic ≡_Bool barrier) For tautVar0 := .disj (.var 0) (.neg (.var 0)) and tautVar1 := .disj (.var 1) (.neg (.var 1)): SemEquiv tautVar0 tautVar1 ∧ tautVar0 ≠ tautVar1 ∧ &neg; SimBA tautVar0 tautVar1 ∧ SimBAN 2 tautVar0 tautVar1.

This cleanly separates structural Fla sharing (solvable in P via Jež recompression with 2 universal steps) from semantic equivalence over distinct syntax trees (which requires genuine variable-eliminating BA steps).

Constructive Bug Localization (ATAQUE-08): Complementary to CF compilation, proof_bug_extractor.py implements arXiv:2611.08305 (Li–Li–Ren, STOC 2026) using pysat_cadical195: given a corrupted chain E0, …, Et ending at Et ≡ 1 and any falsifying assignment x* (E0(x*) = false), findBrokenStep bisects [0, t] to return a concrete invalid transition Ei(x*) = false and Ei+1(x*) = true in ≤ ⌈log2 t⌉ evaluations. Across all four corrupted chains in Table 5—including DattaDutta_v1_DeMorgan_fanout2_bug (catching the simultaneous fan-out 2 violation at step 3 in 2 ≤ 3 queries, 1.023 ms) and PHP_3_3_corrupted (catching step 5 in 3 ≤ 4 queries, 18.009 ms)—binary midpoint localization agrees identically with exhaustive parallel local checks.

As an auxiliary empirical diagnostic in proof_bug_extractor_results.json, Kt witness-compression symmetry-of-information (SoI) measurements across n ∈ {6, 8, 10, 12, 14} confirm zero SoI asymmetry (SoI_asymmetry_bits = 0) on schoolbook_mult, goldreich_xormaj3, and random_permutation, whereas keccak_chi_4round exhibits measurable compressibility at small block sizes (output_compression_ratio = 0.125 at n = 6, 0.4062 at n = 8).
